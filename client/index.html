How to play
  • A secret 4-digit code floats over every head. You see theirs, never your own (? ? ? ?).
  • Get close to decode digits. Walls block your view.
  • To strike, just type a living player's 4 digits. They show at the bottom of your screen as you type, and the 4th digit strikes at once. Backspace fixes a typo. You move at 20% speed while typing, and others see TYPING over your head.
  • Wrong guesses are punished. Hide in bushes, under beds, behind curtains, in wardrobes and in dark rooms, standing still.
  • Items: Master Key (opens a red sealed door), Flashlight, Code Shuffle (new code). 2 slots, use with E/R, drop with Q. Hoods mask 2 digits. Stay inside the zone. Last alive wins.
  • Doors open as you walk up; stairs are walked. F uses light switches, wardrobes, vents and the security monitors. Look into mirrors and puddles to glimpse your own digits.
